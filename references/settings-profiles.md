# Settings Profiles (Sony mirrorless)

Baselines to start from, written around a mid-range APS-C body. Adjust for the body in play (full-frame bodies generally allow roughly a stop higher ISO caps for similar noise; burst and buffer limits vary), the actual lens (max aperture, OSS) and the conditions. When the user asks to save a custom variant, store it in their `/topics/camera-gear.md` memory file under "Settings profiles" and note which body it was written for.

## Portrait

- Mode: A (Aperture Priority) or M
- Aperture: f/1.8–f/2.8 (wider for more background separation, narrower for groups)
- Shutter: 1/160s or faster handheld, or per the handheld-focal-length rule
- ISO: Auto, cap around 3200 outdoors and 6400 indoors on APS-C
- AF: AF-S, Eye AF (Human) on, Focus Area: Tracking or Spot
- Drive: Single or Lo continuous for slight variation in expression
- Other: Face/Eye priority in the AF menu; consider -0.3 to -0.7 exposure compensation to protect highlights on skin

## Action / Wildlife

- Mode: S (Shutter Priority) or M
- Shutter: 1/1000s–1/2000s depending on subject speed
- Aperture: widest usable that still gets the subject sharp (often f/5.6–f/8 on telephoto zooms at max reach)
- ISO: Auto, cap raised to 6400–12800 if needed to hold shutter speed
- AF: AF-C, Wide or Zone tracking; Real-time Tracking for erratic subjects where the body offers it
- Drive: Hi or Hi+ continuous, within the body's burst and buffer limits
- Other: consider electronic shutter for higher fps if there's no flicker or rolling-shutter risk; back-button focus is recommended for action

## Low-light / Astro

- Mode: M
- Aperture: widest available on the lens
- Shutter: per the crop-adjusted 500 rule for stars (500 / (stated focal length × crop factor)); much slower, on a tripod, for static night scenes
- ISO: 1600–3200 as a starting point for astro; adjust after checking the histogram
- AF: manual focus, magnified live view on a bright star or light for infinity
- Drive: Single, 2s timer or remote trigger to avoid shake
- Other: IBIS off on a tripod; consider a star tracker for long astro exposures

## Video (general handheld)

- Mode: Movie mode, M or auto exposure per preference
- Shutter: roughly 1/(2× frame rate) for natural motion blur (e.g. 1/60s at 30fps)
- Aperture: per the desired depth of field; use an ND filter if it's too bright for a slow shutter at a wide aperture
- ISO: Auto within a capped range to avoid visible noise in shadows
- AF: AF-C with Face/Eye tracking for talking-head or subject-following shots
- Other: SteadyShot "Active" (or the body's equivalent) when the lens has OSS and the body has IBIS; watch for focus breathing on the specific lens in use

## Street / walk-around

- Mode: A or P
- Aperture: f/4–f/5.6 for reasonable depth of field and forgiving focus
- Shutter: set an auto minimum shutter speed to avoid motion blur on people
- ISO: Auto, cap around 6400 on APS-C
- AF: AF-C, Wide or Zone; Eye AF on for candid portraits
- Drive: Single, occasionally Lo continuous for decisive-moment sequences
